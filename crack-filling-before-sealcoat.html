Crack Filling Before Sealcoating: Linear vs Alligator Cracks

Water entering an unsealed crack freezes, expands, and washes fine gravel out of the base underneath. Yet not all cracks respond to the same repair—knowing whether a crack is surface shrinkage or structural failure saves wasted sealer.

Crack patternLikely causeAppropriate pre-seal step
Isolated linear cracks (under ~1/2 inch)Normal thermal expansion/contraction and agingClean dry, remove weeds, fill with flexible rubberized crack filler before sealing
Interconnected "alligator" meshSoft, wet, or undersized base unable to support wheel loadsCut-out full-depth patch or base reconstruction; thin sealcoat alone will re-crack rapidly
Edge crumbling along lawnLack of lateral shoulder support or vehicle tires cutting cornersCompact edge support / hot-mix patch before surface sealing
